We spent 9 nights in Malaysia across three very different places: the big-city energy of Kuala Lumpur, the slow, laid-back charm of Ipoh, and the food-and-beach combination of Penang. This is the exact route, with real costs in ringgit and the small logistics that made the trip smooth.

**Route:** Kuala Lumpur (2 nights) - Ipoh (3 nights) - Penang (3 nights) - Kuala Lumpur (1 night before the flight)

Have 10 nights? Add the extra one in Penang. You will not regret it.

## Phase 1: Kuala Lumpur - towers, street food and night life
{: .phase-heading}

### Getting in (do it this way)

- Land at KL airport. **Do not buy a SIM at the airport** - the whole airport has free wifi, so you are covered.
- Take the metro from the airport to KL Sentral station. The views on the way in are genuinely beautiful.
- At KL Sentral, buy a **CelcomDigi SIM - RM 10 for 7 days and 10 GB**. You can top it up later through the app or at any 7-Eleven.
- From Sentral, take a Grab to your hotel.

### Day 1: The city circuit

Do this with the hop-on hop-off city bus, or just Grab between stops - both work.

- **Petronas Twin Towers** - much better at night. If you want the top-floor observation deck, book tickets in advance; they sell out.
- **KL Tower** - home to the highest revolving restaurant.
- **KLCC Park** - the fountain show runs at 8, 9 and 10 PM, and it is a 5-minute walk from the twin towers. Time it so you catch the towers lit up and the fountains in one go.
- **Aquaria KLCC** - one of the biggest aquariums in Southeast Asia.
- **Jalan Alor** - the street food stretch. Go hungry.
- **Berjaya Times Square** - a huge mall if you want a break from the heat.

### Day 2: Caves and Bukit Bintang

- Morning: **Batu Caves**. Go early before the heat and the crowds.
- Evening: **Bukit Bintang** - the city centre for night life, malls and street shows. We ended the night bar hopping and playing pool.

## Phase 2: Ipoh - the slow middle
{: .phase-heading}

Take a bus from KL to Ipoh. It is a laid-back old town and the perfect change of pace after two full-speed days in the capital. Three nights sounds like a lot for a small town - it is exactly right.

- **Rent a scooter** - the easiest way to get around.
- **Limestone cave temples** - the caves around Ipoh are the main draw and worth half a day.
- **Kellie's Castle** - an unfinished colonial mansion with a strange history.
- **Artist's lane** - wall paintings and murals, easy to combine with a cafe stop.
- **Market Street** and the old town - wander, no plan needed.
- **Night market** - good for shopping and snacks.
- **Biscuit shops and cafes** - Ipoh is full of them, and the coffee culture is real.
- **The famous frozen beer** - have at least one. You will understand.
- **Bulgaria Point** - for a view over the whole city.

## Phase 3: Penang - George Town and Batu Ferringhi
{: .phase-heading}

### Getting there from Ipoh (take the ferry, not the cab)

- Bus from Ipoh's **Amanjaya terminal** to **Butterworth terminal**.
- The ferry terminal is inside the bus terminal - a 2-minute walk.
- **Ferry to George Town: RM 2 per person, 15 minutes.** A cab for the same crossing costs around RM 45 and takes 35 minutes. The ferry is the obvious choice, and the harbour views are free.

### Where to stay

We split the three nights between **George Town** and **Batu Ferringhi**, and would do it the same way again:

- **George Town** for food and party. Do not miss the **Gurney Drive night market** for dinner.
- **Batu Ferringhi** for the beach - the stay is very close to the sand - and its own night market. We found surprisingly good Turkish pizza there.

### What we did

- **Rented a scooter and rode a full loop of Penang island.** This was the highlight - the coastal stretches are really beautiful. Rentals are available right in front of the Bayview resort at RM 50 for the day.
- **Entopia butterfly farm** - RM 75 for foreigners. Worth it if you like this kind of thing.
- Beach time, night markets, and a lot of eating.

## Phase 4: One last night in KL
{: .phase-heading}

Head back to KL the day before your flight. Keep it low key: a relaxed dinner, one last walk down Jalan Alor, and souvenir shopping.

### Getting to the airport

- We took a **Grab from KL Sentral to the airport - around RM 67**. With luggage and a flight to catch, the convenience was worth it.
- The cheaper option: the **bus from KL Sentral at RM 15**, running every half hour with multiple operators. Pick based on your budget and timing.

### Claim your VAT refund

If you shopped, claim the tourist VAT refund at the airport: it is a 5-minute walk after immigration, near gates D4-D10, right beside the King Power pickup counter. Keep your receipts handy.

## What things cost
{: .phase-heading}

All prices in Malaysian Ringgit (RM 1 is roughly INR 19-20).

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| CelcomDigi SIM (7 days, 10 GB) | RM 10 |
| Ferry, Butterworth to George Town | RM 2 per person |
| Cab, Butterworth to George Town | ~RM 45 |
| Scooter rental in Penang | RM 50 per day |
| Entopia butterfly farm (foreigner price) | RM 75 |
| Bus, KL Sentral to airport | RM 15 |
| Grab, KL Sentral to airport | ~RM 67 |

### The three tips that saved us the most

1. **Skip the airport SIM.** Free wifi covers the whole airport, and the RM 10 CelcomDigi SIM at KL Sentral costs a fraction of the airport counters.
2. **Take the Butterworth ferry to George Town.** RM 2 versus RM 45 for the cab, and it is the nicer way to arrive.
3. **Book the Petronas top-floor tickets before you land.** They sell out, and the night view is the whole point of going up.
